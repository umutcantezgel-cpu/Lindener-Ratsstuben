# 09 - Landing Page Strategies (Lindener Ratsstuben Localized)

This document formalizes the landing page strategy specifically for the Lindener Ratsstuben single-page / multi-page Gastronomy application. The 36 generic archetypes (SaaS, Web3, Dashboards) have been discarded to preserve the high-performance local SSOT.

## The Active Strategy: Gastronomy High-Conversion Standard

The Lindener Ratsstuben website strictly adheres to a hospitality-focused conversion sequence, designed to reduce friction for the two primary UX intents: Viewing the Menu and Reserving a Table.

### 1. Structural Ordering Logic
For the main landing progression, the following sequence is absolute and mathematically mapped to psychological conversion:

1. **Hero-Centric Visual (First Impression)**
   - Full bleed visual or subtle parallax of the restaurant ambiance.
   - Minimal text ("Rustikale Eleganz" style).
   - High-impact dual CTA: "Tisch Reservieren" (Primary, `#8C1A27`) and "Speisekarte" (Secondary, `#E8B830`).
2. **Value Pillar / About (Trust Building)**
   - Short narrative block emphasizing tradition and quality.
   - Fluid typography (Garamond) to slow the reading pace.
3. **Core Feature: Speisekarte (Menu Preview)**
   - Tabbed or accordion-based menu categories (Vorspeisen, Hauptspeisen, Getränke) to avoid overwhelming long-scrolls.
   - Price alignment and clear dietary markers.
4. **Social Proof & Ambiance (Gallery/Reviews)**
   - High-margin masonry gallery or review block.
   - Lazy loading is critical.
5. **Footer / Conversion Anchor (Logistics)**
   - Contact info, Google Maps iframe, Opening hours, Form/CTA anchor.

### Compliance Directive
Do not insert irrelevant B2B elements like "Comparison Tables (Us vs Them)" or "Pricing Tiers" into this ecosystem. The pipeline strictly enforces the Gastronomy funnel.
